How to Find FDDs

Free State Databases

Several states require franchisors to register and make FDDs publicly available:

  • Wisconsin: Best free source for FDDs
  • California: Comprehensive franchise database
  • Minnesota: Commerce Department database
  • Indiana: Free FDD access

Direct from Franchisors

You can request FDDs directly from franchise companies. By law, they must provide it to serious prospects.

Paid Services

Services like FranChimp ($167/month) provide access to extensive FDD libraries with instant downloads.
